I'm happy to give a talk on this year's 10th anniversary `CuttingEEG Conference <https://cuttingeegx.org/>`_.
I present outcomes from our `large-scale survey among EEG experts <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-024-01941-5>`_, in memory of the 100th anniversary of the first human EEG recording in 1924 and the launch of a manifesto intiative to improve impact and sustainability of EEG science.
