Workshop: "Aesthetic Experience Decoding with Multi-modal Passive Brain-Computer Interfaces" @ BCI Conference 2024 in Graz

I will be part of a workshop on this years BCI Conference, organized together with my colleague Marc Welter from INRIA Centre Bordeaux, France. The workshop connects experts in the field and will focus on the use of BCIs to decode personal preferences and aesthetic experiences. We discuss how multi-modal data integration, including EEG, fNIRS, ECG, and eye-tracking, can be used to enhance the understanding of aesthetic experiences.

I thank the Guarantor of Brain for granting me a Travel Award.
