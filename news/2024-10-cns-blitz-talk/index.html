Talk @ CuttingEEG in Nijmegen

I'm happy to give a talk on this year's 10th anniversary CuttingEEG Conference. I present outcomes from our large-scale survey among EEG experts, in memory of the 100th anniversary of the first human EEG recording in 1924 and the launch of a manifesto intiative to improve impact and sustainability of EEG science.
