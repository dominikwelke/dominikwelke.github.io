Previously known as the EEG Manifesto, this collective initiative is about designing a set of deontological guidelines aiming to reshape our practices, our institutions, and our scientific culture.

I first presented this initiative at cuttingEEG in October 2024, in the name of our core team.

.. The core values we aim to put forward are:
.. - Validity: Scientific robustness and integrity in the face of rapidly evolving methods
.. - Democratization: Diversity, inclusion, and impact for all
.. - Responsibility: What we do, who it impacts, what it sustains

Read all about it `here <https://cuttingeeg.github.io/EEG101CommunityFramework/>`_  and consider engaging/signing/discussing!
